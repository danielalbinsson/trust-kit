# Voice — Agentic Kit

Public product shell for Eve builders. Register: **technical / peer practitioner**.

## Product names (canonical)

| Term | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Agentic Kit** | Public product / site brand |
| **Aletheia** | Inspector engine + `@danielalbinsson/aletheia-cli` |
| **Kit Certified** | Stamp checklist / badge (not “Kit-stamped” as a noun) |
| **Capability Review** | Named paid offer (title case) |
| **Eve** | Framework name; CLI stays `eve build` |

Avoid a second brand (“Trust Kit”) on UI. “Trust” may appear as category language (“local-first trust”), not as a product name.

## Tone

- Direct, specific, assumes competence
- No emojis, no infantilizing language, max one exclamation per page
- Prefer period / colon over habitual em dashes
- No “not just X, it’s Y” cadence
- No internal jargon on public pages (SKU, WTP, Coup)

## Provenance labels (fixed strings)

- `verified from build`
- `from source — build to verify` (em dash allowed; this is a labeled term)
